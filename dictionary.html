Niflarin · Numbers

Núnúise uses a building-block system. Teens are net (ten) + unit: netnoe = 11, nettow = 12. Twenty is towozer (two + zero); from 21 drop the zero: townoe, towtow… townein. Thirty is rheetozer; thirty-one is rheetozernoe. Dates use day/month/year: noe/netnoe/[year] = 1st November.
